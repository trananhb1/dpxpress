About DP Xpress

A NSW logistics company, operating since 2005, delivering parcels on the last mile with StarTrack as our main partner.

Who we are

DP Xpress is run by DP Phan Pty Ltd, a NSW logistics company operating since 2005.

Today we deliver parcels for courier companies. Our main partner is StarTrack, and we also work with other courier companies.

How we work

Courier companies give us runs. Our delivery drivers and subcontractors deliver them. Our back office handles dispatch, accounts and customer service, so the courier deals with one company for the work.
