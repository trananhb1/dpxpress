Privacy policy

How DP Phan Pty Ltd handles the details you send through this website.

Who we are

This website is run by DP Phan Pty Ltd (ABN 60 102 886 288).

What we collect

We collect the details you type into our forms. For courier enquiries: company name, contact name, email, phone, areas, volume, vehicle type, start date and your message. For driver applications: name, email, phone, suburb, vehicle and licence details, ABN status, availability, experience and your message.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry or application and to contact you about it. We do not sell your details.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you. Contact dpphan@hotmail.com or call 0431 644 769.

Complaints

Contact us first at dpphan@hotmail.com. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
